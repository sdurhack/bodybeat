BodyBeats circuit

Arduino UNO Q with one Modulino Light, six LEDs on a breadboard and a Nicla Vision, wired to the laptop that plays the band. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom.

72 BPM · busy drum pattern · A minor
